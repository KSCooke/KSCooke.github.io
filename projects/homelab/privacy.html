Privacy policy

homelab-backups · last updated 24 October 2026

homelab-backups is a personal tool that copies encrypted backups of Kevin Cooke's home server to Kevin Cooke's own Google Drive. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With your permission, it uses Google's drive.file access, which only covers files the tool itself creates. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.

What it does with that access

  • Writes and reads its own backup files, which are encrypted on the home server before they are uploaded.
  • Collects no personal information, uses no analytics or tracking, and shares nothing with anyone.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the home server and is never transferred elsewhere.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services. Deleting the backup folder in Google Drive removes all of its data.
